Backend (app/)

app/db/audit_repo.py: Logs & severity levels.

app/db/__init__.py: DB module exports.

app/services/fleet_sync.py: Manual sync & errors.

app/services/__init__.py: Service module exports.

app/routes/tracking.py: Track & vessel APIs.

app/routes/history.py: History & delete APIs.

app/routes/logs.py: Audit logs & sync APIs.

app/routes/__init__.py: Route module exports.

frontend/index.html: Multi-view HTML layout.

frontend/app.js: UI logic & API calls.

frontend/map.js: Leaflet sea map.

frontend/styles.css: Custom UI styling.
